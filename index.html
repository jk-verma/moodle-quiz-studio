1. Import a question paper

Drop a .docx or .pdf file here, or

Recommended document structure: Q1. Question text; A) Option; B) Option; C) Option; D) Option; E) Optional extra option; Answer: B; Marks: 2; Feedback: explanation. Answers may also be written as Correct option: B. Image-only scans require OCR and are not supported in this version.

Extraction is best-effort. Verify every question, answer, mark, feedback and figure placement before exporting. DOCX preserves inline images; PDF extraction may lose complex reading order. PDF figures are detected from page rendering and placed near their associated question or option. Review every detected figure and placement.

Click an editor first, then Insert on an image

You can also paste a screenshot/image (Ctrl+V), or use Browse image on any question or option. For PDFs, captured page images may include text, so crop externally if necessary.

2. Review and customize

Questions, answer options, option feedback and general feedback each have a rich-text editor with image browsing and clipboard paste. Click inside an editor before pasting or inserting an image. Images can be moved using cut/paste or drag within the editor; select an image to resize or align it using the toolbar.

Client-side conversion. Your documents are processed in your browser. External JavaScript libraries are loaded from CDNs; internet is needed on first load.
